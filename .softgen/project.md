# E-TAXI Košice

## Vision
Professional taxi service website for Košice. Target: people in a hurry needing quick, reliable transportation. Mobile-first experience (most bookings from phones). Core action: instant call or form booking.

## Design
Transit signage aesthetic — Vienna U-Bahn clarity meets Slovak taxi service.

**Colors (HSL format):**
- `--primary: 248 62% 27%` (deep indigo #282462 — brand anchor)
- `--background: 0 0% 100%` (pure white)
- `--foreground: 240 10% 15%` (near-black text)
- `--accent: 30 90% 55%` (warm orange — call-to-action only)
- `--muted: 240 5% 96%` (cool light grey — backgrounds)

**Typography:**
- Headings: **IBM Plex Sans** (600, 700) — geometric, systematic, European transit feel
- Body: **Inter** (400, 500) — high legibility, mobile-optimized
- Data/numbers: `tabular-nums` on phone numbers, prices

**Style direction:**
Geometric blocks, high contrast, generous whitespace between action zones. Single warm accent (orange call button) against cool indigo/grey palette. No gradients, no decorative elements — clarity for time-pressed users.

## Features
1. **Fixed Header** — Always-visible call button (+421 911 606 206) + logo
2. **Hero + Booking Form** — Immediate action: phone or form
3. **Fleet Section** — Vehicle cards with filters (capacity, type)
4. **Footer** — Contact, hours, service area
